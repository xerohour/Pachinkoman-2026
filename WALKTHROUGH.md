# Pachinko Man — Complete Walkthrough

> **Spoiler warning:** this guide reveals every puzzle solution and both endings.
> No complete walkthrough existed anywhere online, so this one was
> reconstructed directly from the game's own level data. It covers the full
> critical path to the good ending, all 8 inventory items, every death/fail
> state, and the optional content.

**Original game:** *Pachinko Man* (a.k.a. *Pachinkoman: A Sphere, Cubed*) by
[Punch the Moon](https://punchthemoon.com/pachinkoman/) — a surreal,
hilarious point-and-click adventure. You play a pachinko-addicted salaryman
who is transformed into a pachinko ball and banished to **Ball Hell**, where
he must toil for the demon king **Baal** — and find a way to escape.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Arrow keys (or WASD, toggled in Options) | Move |
| Left-click / Space | Interact, talk, advance dialogue |
| Hold right mouse button | Walk toward the cursor |
| *On phones:* tap | Same as left-click |
| *On phones:* joystick (bottom-left) | Same as arrow keys |
| *On phones:* OK button (bottom-right) | Same as spacebar |

**Good to know:** deaths are never game-ending — you just respawn. But your
**death count is tracked** and judged at the finale, so a zero-death run
earns special praise.

## Walkthrough

### 1. Your Desk in Ball Hell (`cubicle`) — grab the Stapler and Scissors

After the intro, you wake up at your new desk. Two essential items sit on it:
the **Stapler** and the **Scissors** — but you can't reach them yet.

1. Click the **Office Chair** and choose **Sit On Chair**.
2. Choose **Adjust Chair** to raise it.
3. Now click the **Stapler** → *"A Stapler! I should probably carry this with
   me in case I run into any paperwork-related obstacles."* (Baal chimes in
   that it once belonged to Moloch, demon-god of human sacrifice. It was used
   for *"PAPERWORK, MOSTLY."*)
4. Click the **Scissors** to take them too.
5. **Adjust Chair** to lower it, **Get Off**, and leave through the **Exit**
   on the right → `alley`.

(The Computer offers joke options — Surf the Net, Games, Bash Shell — all
dead ends with gags.)

### 2. The Alley (`alley`) — blind the Guard Demon

A Guard Demon patrols here. If he catches you in his patrol zone, you're
dragged back to your cubicle (*"WEOO WEEOO LACK OF WORKING DETECTED IN
SECTOR 66, ESCORTING BACK TO CUBICLE ASAP"*). Get close to him and **use the
Stapler on the Guard Demon**:

> *"GUAAAGH WHERE DID EVERYBODY GO"*
> BAAL: *"YOU'VE BLINDED MY POOR GUARD DEMON… AN IMPRESSIVE OPENING GAMBIT!"*

His danger zone is now harmless. (Do **not** use the Scissors on him —
*"those TINY ROUNDED SCISSORS WILL NOT AVAIL YOU"* — you'll just get thrown
back.)

Head right → `watercooler`.

### 3. The Water Cooler (`watercooler`) — get the wig

Talk to **Pinball Man** (this sets him as "met"), then **use the Scissors on
Pinball Man** and choose **"Cut Pinball Man's hair"**:

> *Snip snip snip…* **PINBALL MAN'S HAIR has joined the party!**

You're now wearing his hair as a **wig** — you'll need it to get into the
ladies' bathroom later.

Optional here: use the **Mug** (once you have it) on the **Water Cooler** to
fill it with demon tears.

Go to the **Hallway** → `hallway`.

### 4. The Hallway (`hallway`) — the Hate Mail quest

Talk to **Tony the Janitor**. He'll tell you about the Supply Thief who stole
his **crowbar** — the only tool that opens the elevator — and hand you the
**Hate Mail** addressed *"To: The Scandalous Supply-Snatchler."*

Pick up the **Length of Rope** from the floor (optional — it's only good for
jokes).

Go through the **Hole in the Wall** → `presentation`.

### 5. The Presentation Room (`presentation`) — get Murmur's Mug

Walk over the bug **Gregorie**. Murmur the presenter will reward your
"heroism":

> *"…to slay this great hellbeast… such a feat is worthy of an artifact of
> REAL ANCIENT POWER! …oh. You didn't mean to slay him, then. Well, in that
> case I shall bestow upon you… this coffee mug!"*

You got **Murmur's Mug**. (Do **not** use the Scissors on Beelzoten or
Murmur, and don't accuse Murmur of the theft — all fatal.)

Return to the hallway, then back through the alley to the watercooler side…
actually simpler: hallway → ExitDoor → `watercooler` → Downhill → `alley`.

### 6. Back in the Alley — unmask the Supply Thief

**Use the Hate Mail on Morven** and answer **Yes** when he asks if you mean
to deliver it. Pachinko Man lays out the deduction — the thief is Morven
himself:

> *"I am Morven, greatest petty thief in all the underworld!!"*

His own gloating summons the (stapled) Guard Demon for "SLOUCHING," and
Morven is hauled off to justice. (`mail=over` — the quest is complete.)

Enter **Morven's Cubicle** → `morvencubicle`.

### 7. Bathroom detour — recruit the Tragic Eight Ball

You'll need a helper for Morven's pressure plates. From the hallway, go
through the **Bathroom Door** ("LADIES ONLY") **while wearing the wig**:

> *"Ohh, who is that pretty lady?"*

Inside, talk to the **Tragic Eight Ball** (it only joins you now that
`mail=over`) and choose **"Take the Eight Ball with you."** It will follow
you from here on.

While you're here, you can also:
- Shove the Eight Ball into the **Blood Spout** plumbing to plug the leak
  (*"THRUST ME DEEP INSIDE THE FROTHING, BLEEDING WOUND OF DESTINY!"*).
  Melissa will then give you the **Lens of Insight** (optional but useful).
- Fill the **Mug** at the Blood Spout (toilet blood — for the optional
  Cerballus chain).

### 8. Morven's Cubicle (`morvencubicle`) — the pressure plates

Two pressure plates (**SwitchA**, **SwitchB**) guard the trap door to the
Thief's Den. With the Eight Ball following you:

1. Click a switch and choose **Yes** to leave the Eight Ball parked on it.
2. Stand on the other switch yourself.
3. Click **Morven's PC** → *"Entry sequence engaged. Welcome, Morven! Would
   you like to visit the THIEF'S DEN today?"* → **Yes** →
   *"Please hold as we retract the trap door directly beneath your feet."*
   → `thiefsden`.

### 9. The Thief's Den (`thiefsden`) — the printer gauntlet

The Eight Ball warns you: *"THE ORDER IN WHICH YOU EXECUTE TASKS DOWN HERE
IS EXTREMELY IMPORTANT…"* Do these **in order**:

1. **Use the Mug on the Sink** → fill it with **squid ink**.
2. **Use the Mug on the Ink Cartridge** → fill the cartridge. **Do this
   BEFORE cutting any wire**, or the ink won't take.
3. **Use the Scissors on the Wires** → cut **Red first, then Blue**.
   **Never cut Green.**
4. Click the **Control Panel** → **Print**. If the sequence is right:
   *"Bzzzzzz! CHNK-CHNK-CHURRRR… Your print job is now being attended to!"*
   (Wrong order → *"Engaging DOOMSDAY PROTOCOL"* → death by exploding
   printer. The panel's **Reset** wipes your wire/ink progress if you mess
   up.)
5. Pick up the **Crowbar**: *"YOU PICKED UP THE CROWBAR! THIS THING RADIATES
   WITH PRAGMATIC USEFULNESS. ALSO TETANUS. PROBABLY."* — this awakens the
   **Doom Sphere**. Don't touch it (*"THE DOOM SPHERE FLATTENS YOU INTO A
   PACHINKO-PANCAKE"* — and it steals your crowbar).
6. **Use the Crowbar on the Sigil** — smash straight through it:
   *"OH FOR THE LOVE OF — REALLY? …FINE! …JUST SAUNTER ON OUT OF THERE."*
   → escape back to `morvencubicle`.

(The Sigil's glyph-pressing options are red herrings with random responses.
The terminals Mandy/Shanty/Blandy 1000 are pure printer-gossip comedy.)

### 10. The Elevator (`hallway` → `elevator`)

Back in the hallway, **use the Crowbar on the Elevator doors**:

> *"FINALLY! The elevator's open! Time to hightail it out of here!"*

Ride it down → `baalroom`. (Tony gets a wistful farewell: *"…you know, he
could've given me my crowbar back before leaving."*)

### 11. Baal's Room (`baalroom`) — the finale

**Use the Crowbar on Baal.** Three times:

1. **First hit** — *"THWAK"*: Baal is a paper-mâché head containing
   **Tanaka-san**, your real-world boss! The whole thing was *"this
   involuntary anti-gambling, pro-synergy company training exercise."* He
   judges your death count (zero deaths → *"entirely perfect… promoted to
   Employee of the Week"*), then tells you to take the elevator back to
   normal duties.
2. **Do NOT obey him.** The **Elevator** and the **ExitZone** both lead to
   the **bad ending**.
3. **Second crowbar hit** — Tanaka threatens unemployment.
4. **Third crowbar hit** — the *real*, tiny Baal is revealed:
   *"I AM THE GRAND AND POWERFUL BAAL! …YOU'VE BECOME THE FIRST PRODUCTIVE
   EMPLOYEE I'VE EVER HAD! …YOU CAN GO BACK TO YOUR OLD LIFE… OR STAY, AND
   WE CAN RULE BAAL HELL TOGETHER AS PALS. PICK YOUR POISON!"*
5. Pick any option (even *"Stay, on the condition that it is opposite
   day"*) → *"WOW! OKAY! …FINE. I'LL SEND YOU BACK"* → **good ending**.

(Avoid Baal's fists — *"DOWN LOW — TOO SLOW! …'CAUSE I SKWOOSHED YOU."*)

## All 8 inventory items

| Item | Where found | Required use | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stapler | Cubicle (chair raised, while sitting) | Alley: Guard Demon | Staples his eye shut; his patrol zone stops dragging you back |
| Scissors | Cubicle (same) | Water Cooler: Pinball Man; Thief's Den: Wires | Haircut → wig (bathroom entry); cut Red then Blue wires (printer) |
| Hate Mail | Hallway: Tony the Janitor | Alley: Morven (answer **Yes** to accuse) | Morven arrested; unlocks Eight Ball recruitment |
| Length of Rope | Hallway floor | — (jokes only) | Optional |
| Murmur's Mug | Presentation: step on Gregorie the bug | Thief's Den: Sink + Ink Cartridge | Squid ink → filled cartridge (required for the printer); also holds tears/blood |
| Lens of Insight | Bathroom: Melissa (after plugging the leak) | Lobby: Origami-tan | Mind-reading; reveals the dragon's Wi-Fi password (optional chain) |
| Soul Ankh | Presentation: Murmur (after freeing the IT guy) | Thief's Den: Sigil | Joke — the Ankh Spirit refuses to help |
| Crowbar | Thief's Den (after printing) | Den Sigil; Hallway Elevator; Baal ×3 | Escape the den; open the elevator; trigger the finale |

## Deaths and fail states (and how to avoid them)

Deaths respawn you — they only count against Tanaka's judgment.

| Cause | Where | Avoidance |
|---|---|---|
| Guard Demon catches you | Alley patrol zone | Staple his eye first |
| Printer wrong sequence | Thief's Den control panel | Ink → cartridge (before wires) → Red → Blue → Print; never Green |
| Touching the Doom Sphere | Thief's Den | Don't touch it (it also steals the crowbar) |
| Feeding Cerballus tears-only or blood-only | Cavern | Only feed the full blood mixture (tears + blood) |
| Walking into the DeathZone unsubdued | Cavern | Feed Cerballus first |
| Scissors on Cerballus | Cavern | Don't |
| Talking to or using items on Drachenroe | Dragon's office | Only use the Router, with the password |
| Stabbing Beelzoten | Presentation | Don't |
| Attacking or accusing Murmur | Presentation | Don't |
| Touching Baal's fists | Baal's room | Keep your distance |
| Using the Elevator / ExitZone in Baal's room | Baal's room | That's the **bad ending**, not a death — hit Baal with the crowbar 3× instead |

## Endings and unlockables

- **Good ending** (`good-end`): third crowbar hit on Baal → accept his offer.
  Party scene with the whole cast. **Unlocks Cage Match mode** (harder
  difficulty). Beating Cage Match **unlocks Mirror Mode**. The Mirror Mode
  ending hides Murmur's riddle: *"This isn't an ending… Pursue curiousity.
  Don't use the item you think you're expected to use. Follow the rivulets.
  Forsake the river. Do this, and you will unlock the true secret of
  Pachinko Man."*
- **Bad ending** (`bad-end`): obey Tanaka and walk into the elevator/exit.
  *"Whew! What a harrowing day… Hmm. Is it me, or are things a bit…
  Rounder than they were before?"* Baal insists: *"THE ENDING YOU JUST
  UNLOCKED… WAS THE BEST ONE! YEP, NO REASON TO EVER PICK THIS THING UP
  AGAIN."*

## Optional content worth seeing

- **The Cerballus chain** (entirely optional): mix demon tears (Water
  Cooler) and toilet blood (Bathroom spout) in the Mug → feed the full
  blood mixture to Cerballus in the cavern → cross to the **lobby** → use
  the **Stapler** on Origami-tan to bring her to life → use the **Lens of
  Insight** on her to learn Drachenroe's Wi-Fi password, **ProudCatDad73**
  → enter it at the **Router** in the dragon's office (never talk to the
  dragon himself) → the IT guy is freed → collect the **Soul Ankh** from
  Murmur.
- **Lens of Insight mind-reading**: 24 unique inner monologues across the
  cast (Pinball Man's is a guitar solo).
- **The haunted vending machine** (lobby): permanently haunted — attacking
  it triggers a Pokémon-battle parody.
- **Tanaka's judgment** has five variants depending on your death count
  (0 / 1 / 2–5 / 6–20 / 20+).
- **Baal's finale speech** recaps your deeds — with embellishments.
- Cubicle computer (Surf/Games/Bash), the Thief's Den terminals, and the
  Sigil glyphs are all pure comedy with no mechanical effect.
